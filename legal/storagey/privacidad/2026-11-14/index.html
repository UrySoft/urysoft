Las transferencias de los proveedores se rigen por sus condiciones y las garantías aplicables, que pueden incluir decisiones de adecuación y cláusulas contractuales tipo. La relación de cuenta e instalación y el historial necesario de suscripción se conservan mientras sean necesarios para prestar o recuperar el servicio, resolver incidencias y cumplir obligaciones legales. Al dejar de ser necesarios se suprimirán o bloquearán según corresponda. Puedes solicitar la supresión; cancelar la renovación en PayPal no elimina por sí solo los registros que deban conservarse.

Preferencias y comentarios

La web guarda idioma y apariencia en el almacenamiento local del navegador. El acceso de Google utiliza mecanismos técnicos de seguridad. Los comentarios que envíes contienen tu alias, mensaje y valoración opcional: se registran para moderación y solo se publican tras aprobación, junto con las respuestas de UrySoft. Data that stays on your phone

Selected contacts, app shortcuts, background images and widget settings are used on the device and are not sent to the subscription API. Information you voluntarily send by email or in comments is separate from this local data.

Identity and subscription

If you choose Premium, Google returns a signed account identity. The server verifies it and stores a hash of the account identifier, not your profile or Google token. We do not request access to Gmail, Drive or Google contacts. We also store pseudonymized installation identifiers, the link to your PayPal subscription, status and paid period, verification dates and evidence of acceptance of the terms when purchasing is enabled. A hash remains personal data when it links your account to the subscription.

Purposes and legal bases

Managing a purchase, activating and restoring Premium and handling requests relies on performing the contract or steps you request before entering it. Abuse prevention and security rely on the legitimate interest of protecting the service. Information needed for invoicing and tax obligations is processed under legal obligations. We do not use this data for personalized advertising or to sell profiles.

Payments, hosting and external services

PayPal processes payments and financial data under its own policy. We do not store card numbers, banking credentials or your Google or PayPal password. Cloudflare hosts the website and server; the subscription database is configured in the EU. Web connections and providers may process IP addresses, browser information and technical logs. An EU database does not mean that every service operates exclusively in the EU. Provider transfers are subject to their terms and applicable safeguards, which may include adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses. Provider policies: Google · PayPal · Cloudflare.

Retention

Access links expire after 15 minutes; expired records are deleted when new links are created. Temporary security counters are cleaned up during requests. Account and installation links and necessary subscription history are retained while needed to provide or restore the service, resolve issues and meet legal obligations. Once no longer needed, they will be deleted or restricted as appropriate. You can request erasure; canceling PayPal renewal does not itself erase records that must be retained.

Preferences and comments

The website stores language and appearance preferences in browser local storage. Google sign-in uses technical security mechanisms. Submitted comments contain your nickname, message and optional rating: they are recorded for moderation and published only after approval, alongside UrySoft replies. Publication relies on the permission you give in the form. You can request removal.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability where applicable by writing to urysoftcompany@gmail.com. You can also withdraw permission to publish comments. Identity will be verified proportionately; do not send identity documents unless needed. The general response period is one month, subject to lawful extensions. You can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency. No decisions with legal effects are made about you solely through automated profiling; technical payment verification determines Premium access.
