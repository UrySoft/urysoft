Widget and community data

Web addresses, crops, captures and history are stored on your device. The embedded browser may store cookies for sites you visit. The subscription server does not receive your captures or browsing history. The optional community runs on a separate service: https://minairo-community.uryheavy.chatgpt.site. Publishing shares your widget title, URL, settings and image; check that they contain no private information. Community access and data are managed separately from Premium.

Identity and subscription

If you choose Premium, Google returns a signed account identity. The server verifies it and stores a hash of the account identifier, not your profile or Google token. We do not request access to Gmail, Drive or Google contacts. We also store pseudonymized installation identifiers, the link to your PayPal subscription, status and paid period, verification dates and evidence of acceptance of the terms when purchasing is enabled. A hash remains personal data when it links your account to the subscription.

Purposes and legal bases

Managing a purchase, activating and restoring Premium and handling requests relies on performing the contract or steps you request before entering it. We do not use this data for personalized advertising or to sell profiles.

PayPal processes payments and financial data under its own policy. We do not store card numbers, banking credentials or your Google or PayPal password. An EU database does not mean that every service operates exclusively in the EU. Provider transfers are subject to their terms and applicable safeguards, which may include adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses. Provider policies: Google · PayPal · Cloudflare.

Retention

Access links expire after 15 minutes; expired records are deleted when new links are created. Temporary security counters are cleaned up during requests. Account and installation links and necessary subscription history are retained while needed to provide or restore the service, resolve issues and meet legal obligations. Once no longer needed, they will be deleted or restricted as appropriate. You can request erasure; canceling PayPal renewal does not itself erase records that must be retained.

Preferences and comments

The website stores language and appearance preferences in browser local storage. Google sign-in uses technical security mechanisms. Submitted comments contain your nickname, message and optional rating: they are recorded for moderation and published only after approval, alongside UrySoft replies. Publication relies on the permission you give in the form. You can request removal.
